S.S. KESTREL-9 — CARGO DIVISION

CARGO BAY: OXYGEN SORT PROTOCOL

Mineral-rich rocks drift beyond the cargo bay window. Tractor-grab each one, read its oxygen concentration off the scanner, and drop it into the barrel that matches — five of a kind seals the barrel.

READ A rock's O2 value shows as a fraction, decimal, or percent — they can mean the same thing.
SORT The first rock in an empty barrel sets that barrel's target value.
MATCH Wrong value in an armed barrel triggers a hull-damaging blowout.
EJECT Tap a barrel to jettison its contents. Drag a rock through the open iris to jettison just that rock.
